Common questions

How do daily reminders work?

Turn them on in Settings → Daily reminders. You can pick one or more times a day, and each notification shows that day's affirmation.

How do I add the widget?

On iPhone, touch and hold an empty spot on your home screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Affirmery, and choose a size. It shows today's affirmation.

Can I change the kind of affirmations I get?

Yes. In Settings, choose your focus areas (such as calm, self-worth or work) and a level: Gentle, Steady or Bold. Your feed updates to match. For something matched to right now, tap "How are you feeling?" on the Today screen.

Does Affirmery work offline?

Yes. All 365 affirmations are stored on your device, so you don't need an internet connection.

Is my data private?

Yes. Affirmery has no accounts and collects no personal data. Your favorites, streak and preferences stay on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored on your device, so deleting the app removes all of its data.
